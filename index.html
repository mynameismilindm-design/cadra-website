Runner support

Before you run.

Can I see my runs on the website?

Yes. Sign in with your Cadra account to open Runner Studio. Explore your runs and splits, find and follow other runners, and catch up with your clubs and training plan.

How does worldwide matching work?

Choose Duo and pick a distance or time limit. Cadra searches for another runner looking for that format. When you match, you start on a shared countdown. If nobody matches in the search window, try a ghost race.

How does Cadra decide who wins?

In a distance race, the first runner to cover the chosen distance wins. In a timed race, you compete to cover the most distance before the clock runs out. Both runners can follow the gap during the race.

When can I join a community run?

Check the community event in the app for your city’s scheduled local start. Join a distance division while the lobby is open, then see how your finish time compares with others in that division.

Can I keep my route private?

Ghost Mode hides your real-time map location from other runners. Your opponent can still see the distance, pace and gap they need to race. Read the privacy policy.

Where can I try the new training tools?

Run analysis, race debrief, target pacer, race insights, training calendar, private leagues and club organizer are available to testers in Cadra Preview with preview access enabled. Availability in the store version can differ.
