BEFORE THE START LINE

Good questions.

Do we have to run in the same place?＋

No. Each runner follows their own route. You compete over the same predetermined distance, with distance and average pace shared in real time.

How is a ghost race different?＋

A live race happens against someone running at the same time. A ghost race uses a recorded past run—yours or someone else’s. You race that same distance against the recorded time.

Can beginners and walkers join?＋

Yes. Community runs help walkers, new runners and experienced runners build consistency. Register for a distance in the app and check the local schedule for the daily run at 5.

How do the city rankings work?＋

Complete your registered distance. Your finish time determines your position among people in your city who covered that distance. Results are published in the app.

Where can I get help or delete my account?＋

Email milindmylarappa@cadrarunning.com. For deletion, use our account deletion page or the email request form below.
