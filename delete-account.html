CADRA (Cadra) is the new name for Velocity Live. This deletion page applies to the same app and its accounts under either name. The Google Play listing currently uses Velocity Live. The app is operated by Milind Mylarappa.

You can request deletion of your Cadra account and associated personal data without reinstalling the app.

Request by email

Email milindmylarappa@cadrarunning.com with the subject Cadra account deletion. Include the email address registered to your Cadra account. Sending from that address helps us verify ownership.

Email a deletion request

This button opens your email app; send the email to submit your request. If you cannot access the registered address, explain this in your email. Never send your password or one-time sign-in code.

Request in the app

Open Cadra → Settings → Delete account and confirm your request.

Timeframe

We may verify account ownership before processing the request. Verified requests are completed within 60 days after verification, or sooner where applicable law requires. Submitting a request does not immediately erase your account.

Data covered

Deletion covers your account and associated personal data, including profile information, saved workouts, routes and uploaded media, subject to necessary retention exceptions. Completed deletion cannot be undone.

Retention exceptions

Limited records may be retained where necessary to comply with law, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or document the handling of your request, for as long as required for those purposes. Backup copies may remain until the hosting provider’s backup cycle expires and are not intended for continued ordinary use. We will explain applicable exceptions when responding to your request. Copies other people independently retained remain outside our control.
