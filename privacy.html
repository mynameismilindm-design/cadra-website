Cadra Privacy Policy

Who we are

Cadra (formerly Velocity Live) is a running and community app operated by Milind Mylarappa, based in Bengaluru, India. This policy explains how we collect, use, share and retain information when you use Cadra.

Privacy contact: milindmylarappa@cadrarunning.com

Effective date: 26/10/2026

Information we collect

Account and profile information: your email address, account identifier, authentication information, display name, username, profile photo and profile details you provide. Optional details may include city, age, height, weight, running goals and gear.

Running and fitness information: recorded routes, location coordinates and timestamps, distance, duration, pace, speed, elevation, activity history and derived statistics. Motion or activity-recognition data may assist tracking when supported and permitted.

Community information: posts, workout notes, uploaded photos, comments, reactions, followers, messages, club or challenge participation, race results, reports and any supporting files you submit.

Technical information: information needed to operate and secure the service, such as network requests, IP addresses, authentication records and error information processed by our service providers. We also store local settings and unfinished activity drafts on your device.

Location and device permissions

Cadra uses precise location to record runs and calculate routes and running statistics. During an active recording, location may be collected while the app is in the background or your screen is locked, when the required permissions are enabled. Location may also be requested before a run or for nearby features, weather and city lookup.

You can decline or change permissions in your device settings. Features that depend on these permissions may then be unavailable or less accurate. Finishing a recording stops its tracking session. Pausing a run may leave the location service active while recording of workout points is paused.

If you choose to add a photo, Cadra accesses the camera or selected images as needed. Motion/activity-recognition permissions may be requested for tracking. Permission prompts and in-app disclosures explain relevant access before it is enabled.

How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; record, recover and save workouts; calculate statistics and progress; provide challenges, rankings and racing features; display profiles and community content; deliver messages and notifications; respond to support and deletion requests; and investigate abuse, faults and security issues.

Your optional profile information and fitness data support the features in which you provide or use them. Cadra is a fitness app and is not an emergency response service.

Visibility and sharing

Information you share through profiles, activities, posts, challenges, clubs and rankings may be visible to other users according to the feature and applicable privacy settings. Messages are available to their recipients. Do not assume that every profile field is hidden simply because an activity is private.

Recipients may copy, download or otherwise retain information you share. Changing visibility or deleting content cannot remove copies other people have independently retained.

If you initiate an SOS or location-sharing action, your chosen location may be passed to SMS, WhatsApp or a maps application. These services process information under their own policies. Cadra does not automatically dispatch emergency responders.

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests, protect users or address fraud and abuse.

Cadra does not show advertisements, sell personal data, or use or share personal data for advertising.

Service providers

We use Supabase for services including authentication, database and file storage. Our current primary Supabase project is hosted in Japan. Information may also be processed in other countries where relevant providers operate.

Weather requests use Open-Meteo and include location coordinates. City lookup uses OpenStreetMap Nominatim and includes location coordinates. Map services receive the requests needed to display the relevant map area, which may include your IP address and requested map tiles. These requests are necessary for the corresponding features.

Our public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure the website.

Storage and security

We use account authentication, access controls and other technical safeguards to protect information. No service can guarantee absolute security. Do not share your account credentials.

Some workout drafts, queued uploads and settings are stored on your device so that activities can be recovered or saved after an interruption. Signing out does not necessarily erase every local draft. Removing local app data does not delete information already stored on our servers.

Do not treat Cadra messages as end-to-end encrypted communications.

Retention and account deletion

We retain account information and saved activities to provide the service while your account is active, subject to your deletion requests and any applicable retention requirements. Inactivity alone does not constitute a deletion request.

You can request account deletion in Cadra through Settings → Delete account, or by emailing milindmylarappa@cadrarunning.com with the subject “Cadra account deletion”. Our public deletion instructions are available at https://cadrarunning.com/delete-account.html. We may ask for information needed to verify ownership before acting on a request.

Submitting a request does not immediately erase your account. Verified requests are completed within 60 days after we verify account ownership, or sooner where applicable law requires. Deletion covers the account and associated personal data, including activities and uploaded media, except information we must retain for a stated lawful purpose.

Limited records may be retained where necessary to comply with law, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or document the handling of your request, for as long as required for those purposes. Backup copies may remain until the hosting provider’s backup cycle expires; these copies are not intended for continued ordinary use. We will explain any applicable retention exception when responding to your request. Contact us for information about retention relevant to your account.

## Your choices and requests

You can edit supported profile details, manage available privacy settings, revoke device permissions and request account deletion. Available activity export features let you export supported workout data.

Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of information, restrict or object to certain processing, or withdraw consent. Contact milindmylarappa@cadrarunning.com to make a request. We may need to verify your identity. You may contact your relevant data-protection authority where applicable.

Children and intended audience

Cadra is intended for users aged 13 and over. Children under 13 must not use Cadra. If you are under the age of legal adulthood where you live, use Cadra with permission from a parent or guardian where required by applicable law. A parent or guardian who believes a child under 13 has provided personal data may contact milindmylarappa@cadrarunning.com so we can investigate and remove that information as appropriate.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our features or practices change. We will publish the revised version here with its effective date and provide additional notice or seek consent when required. Contact milindmylarappa@cadrarunning.com with questions about this policy.
